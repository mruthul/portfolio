Common questions

Which software do you use?

We work with Odoo — a complete business software used by millions of users worldwide. It covers billing, stock, accounts, staff, website and much more in one system. We work with both the free Community and paid Enterprise editions, and build custom features on top when needed.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your business size, number of users and the features you need. We understand your needs first and then give you a clear quote.

How long does the setup take?

A small shop can usually start quickly, while bigger businesses with many branches take longer. We share a clear timeline before we begin.

Can I use it on my mobile phone?

Yes. You can check sales, stock and reports from your phone, tablet or computer — from anywhere.

What happens if the internet goes down?

Your billing counter keeps working. Bills are saved and sent to the system automatically once the internet is back.

Is my data safe?

Your data is stored on a secure cloud server and backed up every day, so you never lose your records.

I already use other software. Can I switch?

Yes. We move your customers, products, stock, old bills and balances to the new system and check that every number matches.

Will my staff be able to use it?

Yes. It is simple to use, and we train your cashiers, managers and accountant step by step until they are comfortable.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes. We work with businesses anywhere in the world — setup, training and support are done online, or on-site when needed.
